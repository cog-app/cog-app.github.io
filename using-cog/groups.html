Groups

A group is a set of people you share with together. Share a folder, a calendar or an address book with a group, and everyone in it gets access, including people who join later. Leaving a group, or being removed from it, takes that access away.

Your groups are under Settings > Groups: the ones you own and the ones you belong to.

Creating a group

Type a name under Create group and click Create. You are the group's first owner. Open the group to add people.

Members and owners

Owners manage the group: they rename it, add and remove members, and invite people. Members are in it, and get whatever is shared with it.

  • Add member adds someone by name straight away.
  • Add another group as a member puts a whole group inside this one: sharing with this group shares with everyone in that one too.
  • An owner can make a member an owner, or an owner a member, from the Role column.

To leave a group, click Leave beside your own name. The last owner cannot leave: make someone else an owner first. Only an administrator can delete a group.

Invitations

An owner can invite someone instead of adding them, under Invitations on the group's page:

  • With an email address, Cog emails the invitation, if the server has email set up.
  • Without one, Cog makes a link to send yourself. Anyone with the link can join, so send it only to the person you mean.

Choose whether they join as a member or an owner. Pending invitations are listed with their links; Revoke one and its link stops working.

When someone invites you, the invitation shows at the top of Settings > Groups. Join it, or Decline.

Sharing with a group

Wherever you share, search for the group's name instead of a person's. See Sharing.
